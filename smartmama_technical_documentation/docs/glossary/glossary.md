## Domain Terms

**Community Health Volunteer (CHV)**  
The primary SmartMama field operator who conducts household visits and maternal monitoring.

**Supervisor**  
An operational user who reviews CHV verification and oversees CHVs assigned to them.

**Admin**  
A system-level operational administrator who manages supervisors, assignments, support and appropriate activity monitoring.

**Super Admin**  
A highly privileged administrator responsible for higher-level system configuration and security-sensitive operations.

**Identity verification**  
The process of checking identity information/documentation through the identity-verification workflow.

**CHV documentation verification**  
Administrative review of submitted CHV documentation by an authorised supervisor.

**Consent**  
Permission for the stated processing of a mother's information.

**Risk classification**  
The Random Forest model's categorisation of available maternal information into a risk level. It is decision support, not diagnosis.

**Referral**  
A workflow for directing a mother toward further healthcare attention.

**Least privilege**  
Giving a user only the access required for their responsibilities.

**Audit log**  
A record of relevant security or operational activity.

**Verification state**  
The current state of an identity/document or administrative verification process.

**Assignment scope**  
The set of CHVs, mothers or operational records a user is authorised to work with.
